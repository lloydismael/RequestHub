How ratings and statuses are calculated

Scope & status

All SQRs includes accessible records. Active delivery excludes terminal records and proposals without delivery evidence. Closed reflects completion; Pipeline means planning or no delivery evidence; Removed means cancelled or lost. PM falls back to the reviewer when unassigned.

Attention

Attention includes open Off Track, overdue, At Risk, On Hold, due within 14 days, or missing or inconsistent delivery data. Each project is counted once.

Health

Schedule delays of 1–14 days need action; over 14 days are red. Overall is the worse of schedule and recorded delivery health, with open On Hold needing action. Pipeline and removed projects are not rated. Notes are not converted into scores.
