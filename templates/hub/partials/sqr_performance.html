How to read this dashboard Scope, dates & calculation rules

Scope & ownership

Active delivery includes won proposals or explicit delivery evidence: status, health, progress (including zero), start or target date. It excludes lost/canceled proposals and terminal delivery evidence: completed/cancelled status or health, actual finish or signed completion. Assignment or notes alone do not qualify. All SQRs includes every record you already have permission to access.

Effective PM is the assigned PM, falling back to the reviewer when no PM is assigned. My assigned uses that effective owner; a fallback does not resolve a missing assigned PM.

Dates & attention

Dates use the server-local snapshot date, not your browser clock. Overdue means operationally open with a target before today. Due soon includes today through day 14; the timeline includes today through day 30. No target means unknown, not on time. Open 100% progress does not erase an overdue deadline.

Attention counts each project once: open Off Track, overdue, At Risk, On Hold, due soon, or missing/inconsistent delivery data, prioritized in that order. Delivery candidates missing assigned PM, health, progress, start or target are flagged; ordinary proposals are not. Date order, contradictory terminal signals and completion/progress conflicts are separate inconsistencies.

Health & completion

Health and delivery status are the values recorded in the Tracker; blanks remain Not set. Dates never automatically change health. Updates / risks are recorded notes, not a calculated risk score.

Average progress is the unweighted mean of known values, bounded to 0–100%, in this filtered view. Unknown values are excluded, not treated as zero; no known values means N/A. Coverage shows known values / projects in view. Terminal records contribute only when included by scope and filters. This is reported completion, not schedule performance or a historical trend.

Snapshot time is retrieval time, not the last delivery update. Existing Excel exports cover Tracker / Reports or legacy performance data, not this filtered dashboard.
