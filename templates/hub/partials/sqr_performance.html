How ratings and statuses are calculated

Portfolio & status

The view includes accessible SQR records. Closed reflects completion; Pipeline means planning or no delivery evidence; Removed means cancelled or lost. PM falls back to the reviewer when unassigned. My Assigned follows the reviewer, not the displayed PM.

Attention

Saved amber or red assessments, open On Hold, and missing or inconsistent delivery data may need attention. Each matching project is counted once. Filters and charts use all matches before table pagination.

Assessments

Schedule, Budget/Effort and Quality use the exact saved choices in SQR Delivery > Progress. Red takes priority over amber; all three must be green for Healthy. Partial coverage is Incomplete assessment; zero coverage is Not assessed. Notes and legacy Health do not supply ratings.
